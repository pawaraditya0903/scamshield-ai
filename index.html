      Awareness

      Common Scam Types

      🏦

      Fake Bank Alerts

      "Your account is blocked, click to verify" — Banks never send such messages with links.

      💸

      UPI Fraud

      Fraudsters send collect requests or fake payment screenshots to trick you into sending money.

      🚔

      Digital Arrest

      Scammers impersonate police/CBI via video call and demand money to "clear your name."

      🔑

      OTP Fraud

      "Share OTP for verification" — No legitimate service will ever ask for your OTP on a call.

      🎁

      Lottery/Prize Scam

      "You've won ₹50 Lakhs! Pay processing fee." — If you didn't enter, you didn't win.

      💼

      Job Scam

      "Work from home, earn ₹50K/day" — Unrealistic offers that ask for registration fees are scams.
